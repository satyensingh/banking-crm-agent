# Relationship AI

Your banking copilot for **personal-loan outreach**. Ask in plain language and the assistant uses
read-only CRM tools to:

- find and rank high-potential customers
- spot who is likely to convert **this month**, from recent transaction trends and life events
- explain scores, eligibility and product suitability
- draft WhatsApp messages for customers who have given permission

**Human in the loop:** drafts are for your review and are never sent automatically. Scores are
heuristics, not conversion probabilities, and product matches are preliminary - not credit decisions.
